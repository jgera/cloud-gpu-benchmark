Method and sources

Training profiles

Vision: Ultralytics YOLO26n training on COCO (640px, mixed precision). Transformer: LynxBenchAI DistilBERT BF16 training. Multimodal: An adjustable blend of the two workloads, not a measured multimodal model.

Where a matched GPU result is unavailable, a clearly marked proxy is used. A100 40 GB results are modeled from A100 80 GB throughput at 95.5% and should be treated as provisional.

Cost model

Estimated hours = reference hours × reference-to-4090 factor ÷ relative throughput. Compute cost = hours × price per hour. Extra compute time and fixed charges are optional inputs.

GPU memory is a feasibility filter, not a speed estimate. Results are not a substitute for testing your model.
