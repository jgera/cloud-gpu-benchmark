Methodology and Data Sources

Training Workload Profiles

Vision: Ultralytics YOLO26n training on COCO (640px, mixed precision). Transformer: LynxBenchAI DistilBERT BF16 sustained training results. Multimodal: Weighted harmonic mean of Vision and Transformer reference runtime share ($1 / (\frac{w}{s_v} + \frac{1-w}{s_t})$).

Where direct measurements are unavailable, documented cross-model proxies are used (e.g. MobileNetV3 for 4080-series cards; A100 40 GB modeled at 95.5% of A100 80 GB). All proxies are clearly tagged as Estimated.

Cost & Throughput Formulation

Equivalent runtime is scaled relative to the chosen reference GPU: $\text{hours} = (\text{ref\_hours} \times s_\text{ref} / s_\text{gpu}) \times (1 + \text{overhead}\%)$. Compute cost = $\text{hours} \times \text{hourly\_rate} \times \text{GPU\_count}$. Storage and idle setup hours are factored into total project expenses.

The Pareto frontier isolates non-dominated offers (offers where no alternative is simultaneously faster and cheaper).
