# Separate free-credit opportunities

These programmes are **not included in the paid rental cost calculations**. Their eligibility, quotas, GPU access, and terms may change. Confirm conditions before activating time-limited credits.

| Programme | Publicly advertised benefit | Renewal | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Modal Starter](https://modal.com/pricing) | $30 free compute monthly | Monthly | Account plan allowance; includes GPU, CPU and memory costs |
| [Lightning AI](https://lightning.ai/pricing) | Up to 30 introductory credits | One time | Signup and payment-card requirements; session limits |
| [RunPod for Startups](https://www.runpod.io/startup-program) | $1,000 Starter Tier | Application award | Selective, generally favors new customers with a working product |
| [E2E Networks startup programme](https://www.e2enetworks.com/startup-program) | Advertised startup credit programme | Application award | Eligibility and accepted legal entity must be checked |
| [Vast.ai startup programme](https://vast.ai/article/vast-ai-startup-program) | Advertised startup credits | Application award | Selective application and specific credit terms |
| [Google Cloud free trial](https://cloud.google.com/free) | $300 introductory credits, 90 days | One time | GPU requires billing upgrade and quota approval |
| [Microsoft for Startups](https://startups.microsoft.com/) | Programme credits by eligibility level | Application / verification | GPU quotas and eligible services differ from ordinary free services |
| [NVIDIA Inception](https://www.nvidia.com/en-in/startups/) | Partner benefits and possible credits | Membership benefits | Incorporated product-focused startup requirements |

A few high-value promotions are designed for qualifying startups, not general research notebooks. Do not plan the project budget around credits until the award is approved and a GPU configuration is actually eligible.
